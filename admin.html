QR Code — Customer Self-Order

Print this QR on jar stickers, shop walls, or WhatsApp it to customers. When scanned, it opens your customer-facing order page where customers can place an order in 20 seconds.

Customer Order Page
order.html (open in a separate tab to preview)
Open Order Page
How it works — Customer scans the QR with their phone camera, picks items (Camper, Jar) with quantities, enters name + phone + address, and hits submit. The order appears here in the Orders tab with a "QR" channel tag. You can also stick one QR on every jar delivered, so reordering is one scan away.
